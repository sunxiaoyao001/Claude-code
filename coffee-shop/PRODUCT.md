# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
delegated: static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JS, single `index.html` plus local assets. Chosen because the owner wants something that opens by double-click and can be hosted anywhere without a build step.

## Users
People in the shop's city (neighbours, office workers, weekend wanderers) who are deciding where to go sit with a coffee. They usually arrive on a phone, from a map pin, a friend's link or a social post, and want to know within seconds: what is this place like, what should I order, where is it and is it open now.

## Product Purpose
A landing page for a neighbourhood coffee shop. Success is a visit: the visitor leaves the page knowing the address, today's hours and one drink they want to try, and walks in.

## Positioning
Open decision. This is a demonstration build: the shop is not real yet, so the name, menu, address and story are placeholders authored for the demo and must be replaced with real facts before launch.

## Operating Context
Visitors read on mobile, often outdoors or in transit; the decisive facts are address, opening hours, how to get there, and the signature drinks. The physical shop (counter, seats, light, the people behind the bar) is the product; the page is its front window.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Primary action: come to the shop (directions / map, hours). No online ordering, booking or payment.
- Language: Simplified Chinese first.
- Undecided: real shop name, address, menu, prices, photos, social accounts.

## Evidence on Hand
None. There are no real photos, reviews, press, customers or awards. Do not fabricate testimonials, ratings, press quotes or "best coffee in town" claims. Demo content (name, menu, address, hours) is synthetic and must be labelled as such on the page.

## Product Principles
1. The visit is the conversion: address, hours and directions are never more than one glance away.
2. Show the place and the cup, not adjectives about them.
3. Honest specifics over hype: origins, prices, opening hours, seat count.
4. Fast and readable on a phone in daylight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access; respect reduced-motion preferences.
