# Surface brief: landing page (index.html)

Scope: single-page landing for the demo shop "十二号咖啡 / No.12 Coffee", 梧桐路 12 号. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience & job: locals on a phone deciding where to sit with a coffee. Action: come to the shop (open-now status, hours, one-tap directions).
Proof/content: no real evidence; all names, menu, prices, address and hours are synthetic and labelled as demo on the page.
Constraints: Simplified Chinese, static HTML/CSS/JS, mobile-first daylight reading, WCAG AA, reduced motion.
Roll: degraded (no challengers, roll service unreachable). User chose IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the assigned direction (coffee-sack stencil) and the canon.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shop's address is its brand. The page is the street wall the shop hangs on: cobalt enamel door plates and street-name signs carry the name, the hours and the way in. Refuses the category default of a cosy latte photo hero with menu cards beneath.

OWN-WORLD: Grey-blue 青砖 brick wall as the street ground (hero and 怎么来); the middle sections (价目/豆单/店里) are the shop's interior limewashed 粉墙 with plaster losses showing the same 青砖, so every plate still hangs on a wall; cobalt enamel plates (white inner rule, rounded corners, four slotted screws, small chipped edges showing dark iron) carry all key content; white enamel plates for secondary info; one signal-red enamel plate for live open/closed. Heavy Song (Noto Serif SC 900, self-hosted subset) for plate lettering, Barlow Semi Condensed (highway-sign lineage) for pinyin, numerals and plate codes, the system CJK sans (PingFang / YaHei) for reading text.

STORY: Visitor sees the door plate, learns this is No.12 Coffee at 梧桐路 12 号, sees whether it is open right now, taps the street-sign arrow to navigate; scrolling, reads the enamel price board, today's beans, what the room offers, and how to get there from the metro.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brick wall full-bleed. Top: a long street-name sign strip used as navigation (梧桐路 WUTONG LU with section links). Centre-left, large: the door plate "梧桐路 / 12号" with pinyin, about 60% of the width on desktop and full width on mobile. Hung beside/under it: red two-sided open/closed plate showing live status and today's hours, and a street-sign arrow plate "带我过去 →" as the primary action (map link). A white enamel shop signboard carries the H1 shop name 十二号咖啡 plus the hook 推门就是咖啡 (promoted from a small hook plate: product truth, the visitor must learn the shop's name). On mobile the door plate leads, then the signboard, then the two signs, all within the first viewport. Nav strip's left plate reads 梧桐路 WUTONG LU.

FORM: enamel door plates & street signage, my grounded list position 1 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, chosen by user over assigned position 6); seed key aafa29fa. Signature interaction: the open/closed plate is a real two-sided hanging sign that flips on its hook according to the live time and on tap; plates settle with a small pendulum swing once when first seen.
